About this prototype

  • A photographed room with real-time 3D products in it. The camera was solved from the desk’s four corners, so the products sit in the photo at true scale and cast soft shadows onto it.
  • Built to the brief’s numbers. The mat is 700 × 350 × 3 mm with 12 mm corners. The stand opens to a 110 mm rear height and folds to a 22 mm envelope. Each clip is 44 × 20 × 12 mm with three channels.
  • Every motion is a real product action. The mat arrives rolled and unrolls, the stand opens under the laptop, and at 18:00 it folds flat again.
  • Not included: the laptop, the cable and everything else in the room are there for scale and context only.
  • Images. The room plates, materials and product photos are AI-generated concept images. The lighting comes from a captured interior HDRI (CC0, Poly Haven).
  • Dressed in the locked identity. The folded full stop, Chillax for names and Gentium Book Plus for reading and prices. Felt and silver by day, lamp amber once the stand folds at six.
  • Reduced motion. The camera cuts between set views instead of moving, and pages open without animation.
