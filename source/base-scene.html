About this prototype

  • Real-time 3D, built to the brief’s numbers. The mat is 700 × 350 × 3 mm with 12 mm corners. The stand opens to a 110 mm rear height and folds to a 22 mm envelope. Each clip is 44 × 20 × 12 mm with three channels.
  • The fold is a working mechanism, not a video. The stand’s scissor legs are solved live, so every angle is the same object.
  • Every motion is a real product action. The mat arrives rolled the way it ships and unrolls, and the stand arrives folded and opens. At 18:00 they reverse.
  • Materials and photos. Felt, oak, aluminium, silicone and clip textures are generated tileable maps. The product photos in each page are AI-generated concept renders, labelled as photos.
  • No logo, colours or brand type yet. It’s neutral on purpose, so the experience is judged on its own.
  • Reduced motion. The camera cuts between set views instead of flying, and the product pages open without animation.
